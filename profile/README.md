# NZRT Network

Charitable technology organisation based in Kerikeri, Northland, New Zealand.

We provide free enterprise-grade technology services to NZ charities, community groups, schools, and kura - operated by Nathan Livingstone with 8 Claude AI agents.

## What We Provide (Free for Qualifying Organisations)

- **Knowledge Systems** - Obsidian vaults and AI-ready documentation for organisations that struggle to manage information
- **Claude Code AI Agent Setup** - Multi-agent AI configurations and MCP integrations for community organisations needing automation
- **Self-Hosted Business Stack** - Nextcloud + Dolibarr + WordPress for charities leaving costly SaaS subscriptions
- **Iteasel** - Portable wooden easels for IT display, donated or sold at cost to schools and kura

## Charitable Status

Applying for registration as a charitable company under the Charities Act 2005 (NZ).
